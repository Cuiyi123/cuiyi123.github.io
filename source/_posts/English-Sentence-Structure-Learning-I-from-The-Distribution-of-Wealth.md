## Chapter I Issues that Depend on Distribution

1. For students, importance **attaches to** one economic problem —— **that of** ...

2. There is a law according to **which** the income of society is divided into wages, interest and profits.

3. **As the result of** ..., that <u>which comes to wage-earners</u> is the pay that employers give to them.

4. The amount of this pay fixes ...

5. The money <u>that a man earns</u> may be thought of **as** potential well-being **condensed** into a material form; and **if** workers now get enough of it to put them on a high plane of comfort, their descendants will probably reach ...

6. The amount ... <u>may seem to depend on</u> ...

7. The forces **that** control the ... terms of wage contracts are those **which** determine the amount of that productive power. 

8. The function of this natural law is <u>to seperate A into B</u> that are ...

9. It **causes** the whole annual gains of society **to** distribute themselves **into** three great sums —— A, B, C.

10. It **consists** (entirely) **in** the establishing and maintaining of efficient relations between the agents of production.
